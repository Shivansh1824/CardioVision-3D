CardioVision 3D

Official Architectural Reference: What is Common vs. What is Different between Doctor & Patient Profiles.

🔗 What is 100% COMMON (The Shared Foundation)

These core systems are built once. They live underneath both profiles, ensuring that medical biology remains objective and development time stays efficient.

🫀
Interactive 3D Heart & Torso
Three.js / React Three Fiber Canvas
  • ✓ Identical 3D Model: Both views interact with the exact same high-resolution human anatomical heart.
  • ✓ Full 3D Controls: 360° mouse orbit, pinch zoom, smooth camera pan, and anatomical layer toggle.
  • ✓ Universal Color Coding: Green = Normal (<35%), Amber = Moderate Stenosis (35–65%), Pulsing Crimson Red = Severe Risk (>65%).
🧠
AI Engine & 4 Predictions
Trained on 303 Verified Hospital Records
  • ✓ 4 Multi-Target Models: Overall CAD status, LAD stenosis, LCX stenosis, and RCA stenosis probabilities.
  • ✓ Strict Anti-Leakage: Ground-truth targets (Cath, LAD, LCX, RCA) are never leaked into feature inputs.
  • ✓ Sub-25ms Speed: Instant recalculation so dragging sliders updates the heart colors live without page reloads.
⚡
"What-If" Life-Saving Simulator
Dynamic Real-Time Recalibration
  • ✓ Interactive Sliders: Both can tweak Blood Pressure (BP) and LDL Cholesterol.
  • ✓ Live Visual Shift: Lowering values visually cools down the red glowing artery into green on screen.
  • ✓ Visual Proof of Hope: Directly demonstrates the physical benefit of medication and healthy habits.
🛡️
Safety & Persistence (Supabase)
Regulatory Compliance & Cloud Storage
  • ✓ Mandatory Clinical Disclaimer: Prominent banner stating this is decision support/educational, not an invasive angiogram.
  • ✓ Cloud Database: Both profiles write diagnostic runs and simulation plans to Supabase PostgreSQL.
  • ✓ 1-Click PDF Generation: Both can export a clean, printable document summarizing the findings.

🧭 Scope Guard: What To Do vs. What NOT To Do

Clear boundaries to ensure we win the hackathon in 14 days without wasting a single hour on unnecessary bloat.

✅ WHAT WE MUST DO (High Value)
  • ✓ Unified Single App with a Role Switcher: Build one React app with a top toggle: [🩺 Doctor View] | [👤 Patient View].
  • ✓ Pre-load 303 Hospital Presets: Allow judges to test any patient case with one click.
  • ✓ Train 4 Clean ML Models: CAD, LAD, LCX, RCA with strict feature leakage elimination.
  • ✓ Real-time 3D Shader Glow: Make LAD, LCX, RCA shift smoothly between Green, Amber, and Red.
  • ✓ SHAP Explainability Engine: Explain why each artery is in danger in both clinical and plain-English modes.
  • ✓ Clinical Safety Disclaimer Banner: Prominently displayed on both screens for medical compliance.
❌ WHAT WE MUST NOT DO (Time Traps)
  • ✕ Do NOT build two completely separate websites: It wastes days replicating 3D setup, styling, and database wiring.
  • ✕ Do NOT generate 3D meshes from scratch: The 3D heart is pre-built; we only color-code and animate its arteries.
  • ✕ Do NOT force mandatory email verification on judges: Provide a "1-Click Doctor Demo Login" so judges test immediately.
  • ✕ Do NOT build payment gateways or telemedicine video calling: They add zero points to the hackathon rubric.
  • ✕ Do NOT over-complicate the patient form: Keep the patient input under 4 simple vitals; don't ask them for their BUN or RWMA.
